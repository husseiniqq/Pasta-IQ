Our Story

Italian Soul, Baghdad Heart

"An Iraqi pasta concept inspired by authentic Italian flavors and crafted for modern food lovers."

Pasta IQ began as a cloud kitchen with a simple obsession: bring the rich, comforting flavors of true Italian pasta to Baghdad — without compromise. Every sauce is built from scratch, every cup is portioned by hand, and every order is finished moments before it leaves our kitchen.

From rich red ragùs to silky white sauces, from vegetarian classics to indulgent special recipes, our menu reflects a single idea: pasta should feel like an occasion, even in a cup.
